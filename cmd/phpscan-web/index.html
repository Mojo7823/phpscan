PHP2Shell check

PHP2Shell (CVE-2026-109618, CVE-2026-109619) lets attackers take over servers that run an outdated PHP. Enter your website to see if it is affected.

We open your page once, like a browser visit, and read which PHP version it reports. No attack, no login, nothing is changed or stored.

Routers, modems and other devices on your office network can be affected too. Check them with our free one-time companion tool (open source). Need help? Talk to our security team.
